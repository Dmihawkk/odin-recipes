Lasagna

To prepare a traditional lasagna, you start by simmering a rich meat sauce made with ground beef, onions, carrots, celery, and tomato purée for several hours to develop a deep flavor. While the sauce cooks, you prepare a creamy béchamel by melting butter, whisking in flour to make a roux, and gradually adding milk until the mixture thickens into a velvety consistency. You then begin the assembly by spreading a thin layer of meat sauce at the bottom of a baking dish, followed by a layer of flat pasta sheets and a generous spread of both meat sauce and béchamel. After repeating these layers several times and finishing with a final topping of sauce and plenty of grated Parmesan cheese, you bake the dish in a preheated oven at 180°C until the pasta is tender and the top is golden and bubbling. After taking it out, it is essential to let the lasagna rest for about fifteen minutes so the layers can firm up, making it much easier to slice and serve.

  • 250g Lasagna pasta sheets
  • 500g Ground beef
  • 1 Litre Tomato purée
  • 1 Onion, 1 Carrot, and 1 Celery stick
  • 50g Butter
  • 50g All-purpose flour
  • 500ml Whole milk
  • 100g Grated Parmesan cheese
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Optional: A pinch of nutmeg (for the béchamel) and a splash of red wine (for the meat sauce)
  1. Heat a large pot with olive oil and sauté finely chopped onion, carrot, and celery until softened.
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, then pour in the tomato purée and let it simmer on low heat for at least two hours.
  3. Prepare the béchamel sauce by melting butter in a saucepan, whisking in the flour, and gradually adding milk while stirring constantly until thick and smooth.
  4. Season the white sauce with a pinch of salt, black pepper, and nutmeg for extra flavor.
  5. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F) and lightly grease a large rectangular baking dish
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce at the bottom of the dish to prevent the pasta from sticking.
  7. Place a single layer of lasagna sheets over the sauce, followed by more meat sauce and a generous ladle of béchamel.
  8. Repeat these layers—pasta, meat sauce, and béchamel—until you reach the top of the dish or run out of ingredients.
  9. Finish with a final layer of béchamel and a thick coating of grated Parmesan cheese.
  10. Bake in the oven for about 30 to 40 minutes until the top is golden-brown and bubbling.
  11. Remove the lasagna from the oven and let it rest for 15 minutes before cutting it into squares.
