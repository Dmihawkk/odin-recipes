Amatriciana

To prepare an authentic pasta all'amatriciana, you begin by cutting thick slices of guanciale into small strips and sautéing them in a pan over medium heat until the fat has rendered and the meat is golden and crispy. Once ready, you remove the guanciale and set it aside, using the remaining flavorful fat to briefly sauté a small piece of fresh chili pepper for a touch of heat. You then add high-quality peeled tomatoes, crushing them gently with a fork, and let the sauce simmer for about ten to fifteen minutes until it thickens and develops a rich consistency. While the sauce cooks, you boil a pot of salted water and cook your pasta—traditionally bucatini or spaghetti—until it is perfectly al dente. After tossing the drained pasta directly into the sauce along with the crispy guanciale, you remove the pan from the heat and stir in a generous handful of grated Pecorino Romano cheese to create a creamy, savory finish.

  • 400g Bucatini or Spaghetti
  • 150g Guanciale
  • 400g Peeled tomatoes
  • 75g Pecorino Romano cheese
  • 1 Small fresh chili pepper
  • A splash of dry white wine
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt
  1. Cut the guanciale into uniform strips, about 1 cm thick
  2. Place the guanciale in a large frying pan over medium heat and sauté until the fat has melted and the meat is golden and crispy.
  3. If you like, pour in a splash of dry white wine and let it evaporate completely.
  4. Remove the crispy guanciale from the pan and set it aside, leaving the rendered fat in the pan.
  5. Add the chili pepper and the peeled tomatoes (crushed by hand or with a fork) to the same pan.
  6. Simmer the sauce for about 10 15 minutes until it reaches a thick, rich consistency.
  7. While the sauce is simmering, cook the bucatini in a large pot of boiling salted water.
  8. Drain the pasta a couple of minutes before it's fully cooked (al dente) and add it directly into the sauce.
  9. Add the crispy guanciale back into the pan and toss everything together to coat the pasta evenly.
  10. Remove the pan from the heat, add a generous amount of grated Pecorino Romano, and stir well until creamy.
  11. Serve immediately with an extra sprinkle of Pecorino on top.
